Max Headroom Companion Installed

The browser companion is ready. No Developer Mode or DevTools are required.

  1. Open your Foundry VTT world with FoundryVTT Max Headroom enabled.
  2. While viewing that Foundry tab, click the Max Headroom Relay browser extension.
  3. Click Pair Current Foundry Tab.
  4. In Foundry's Max Headroom Relay Controller, click Open StreamKit.
  5. Configure your friendly Discord-user mappings in Reactive Portrait Configuration.
